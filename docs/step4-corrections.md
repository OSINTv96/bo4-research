# Step 4 corrections and review boundaries

The full independent review of the original collection checked all 1822 assigned
units and requested three corrections. This version carries those corrections.
The first correction review then found a nesting-depth defect in the same
scanner; this second version also fixes that exact finding.
The [correction record](../provenance/step4-corrections.json) pins the original
review and the corrected producers. Final independent acceptance is recorded
separately in the research archive; this page is the correction scope, not a
self-issued approval.

- Boolean AND no longer creates pointer references. Six false connections were
  removed and 123 ordinary calls have their correct form.
- Spaces, newlines and comments around `::` do not admit an unrelated local
  function. The complete graph and the earlier local trace use the same rule.
- Current settings prose consistently reports 773 textual sites and 352 names.
  The original 722-site/332-name census is labeled historical.
- Nested developer-only regions stay active until their outer closing delimiter.
  Three wrong roots and six links are removed, thirteen witness flags are fixed,
  and one retained node has a new valid predecessor. Malformed delimiters stop
  parsing explicitly. Retained node IDs stay the same; removed IDs are reserved.

The regenerated graph has 5991 candidate nodes and 12755 edges. The selected
source files, 97 qualified binding gaps and 126 remaining
context includes are unchanged. Eighteen of the 537 earlier decision records
have corrected witnesses. Source files and historical subjects/reviews stay
unchanged; old claims retain their exact prior scopes and limitations.

Whole-file context and partial contracts are not full behavior approvals.
The 255 unobserved Lua candidates, compiler/VM fidelity, native registration,
effective settings, unavailable services and gameplay remain separate evidence
limits. No runtime or whole-mode completion is added by these corrections.
